Saber toothed tiger

Big Cat

The Saber toothed tiger was once a ferocious predator in the Pleistocene epoch(2.5 million years ago).

They primarily hunted large herbivores such as bison, camels, horses, ground sloths, and young mammoths, using ambush tactics and precision killing.

Saber toothed tigers had long, curved canine teeth that could grow over 7 inches in length! That's 17.78 centimeters long!
